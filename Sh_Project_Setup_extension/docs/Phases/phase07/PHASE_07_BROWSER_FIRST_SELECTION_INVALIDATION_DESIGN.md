## Four independent authority boundaries

| Boundary | What may be established | What it cannot establish |
|---|---|---|
| Browser access | A genuine invocation and a successful bounded read demonstrate usable access for the observed tab/document at that moment | Correct site account, entitlement, continuing access or operation approval |
| Verified website context | Reviewed origin, document, route/page kind and explicit role/target confirmation agree for a bounded read | Authentic account identity or site rights; shared DOM and login appearance are untrusted |
| Account rights | Future separately authorized site-specific evidence and owner policy may establish the intended account's relevant rights | A generic permission to perform all operations; GitHub and Odoo.sh rights remain independent |
| Operation approval | Future immediate, specific human approval of exact target/effect after all other gates | Revival after drift, blanket approval, retries/replay or durable duplicate-write assurance |

In BF-06, `accountVerified` and `executionAuthorized` stay **false** in every state. `CONTEXT_VERIFIED_READ_ONLY` means only bounded page-context agreement; the UI must explain this meaning. Native-site human account/target confirmation is labeled **MANUALLY_ATTESTED**, never automatic verification. A familiar hostname, URL, title, avatar, login appearance or successful script read cannot prove account identity. No page observation or synthetic Phase 06 approval grants execution.

## Intended human selection UX

1. Explain the three roles, existing site sign-ins and four boundaries. Guided observation requires the separately reviewed navigation capability/consent described in BF-04. Denied/unavailable guided observation offers manual mode without widening permissions.
2. The human activates the **specific website tab**, then invokes the extension from that tab. Repeat separately for Plementus, GitHub and Odoo.sh. Merely opening/activating a tab or choosing its ID in a review page supplies no invocation.
3. Candidate future action handling has no default popup and captures Chrome's `action.onClicked` tab ID/window **before** opening/focusing the packaged review page. This is BF-04's prospective UI migration, not an edit to ADR-005's current scaffold. Never query a later focused tab to reconstruct the invoking tab. Chrome's action/popup rules are documented in [C1](#official-chrome-source-register).
4. After a genuine event, obtain only authorized, bounded top-frame metadata. Start with browser-supplied context, exact HTTPS origin checks and document identity; any DOM page-kind read requires a later reviewed packaged read contract. Missing/denied metadata is not a reason to scrape or request broader access. Capture remains unassigned until human choice.
5. In the trusted review UI, the human explicitly assigns/confirms **role and intended target** for that captured tab. Hostname may reject a mismatch; it must never auto-assign a role, including when only one candidate exists. Display a local Tab A/B label, chosen window label, safe origin and state. Show account/target details on the native site; keep customer values and sensitive routes out of the extension's diagnostic display.
6. Check exactly one selection per role, three distinct tab IDs, and distinct reviewed origins as in BF-04. GitHub's expected origin is `https://github.com`; actual Plementus/Odoo.sh origins require separate owner/site validation, not guessed hostnames. Shared-origin deployments are unsupported pending a separate role-isolation decision. A duplicate-origin extra tab does not invalidate an explicit unique selection and is never substituted automatically.
7. Complete the fresh observation transaction below, checking selected tab/window, exact origin and full URL, active top document, current capability and unchanged context generations. Account/target ambiguity blocks the confirmation; human attestation cannot make account rights verified.
8. The review page shows all three roles with their individual states and a global “Read-only context reviewed; account rights unverified” status only when the aggregate predicate holds. Use text, accessible status announcements and keyboard controls; no color-only meaning or preselected approval. Provide “Show selected tab”, “Select again”, “Stop observation” and “Continue manually”.
9. Any uncertainty clears the aggregate result and requires fresh observation through the permitted transitions, fresh reselection or manual fallback. There is no “execute” control in this candidate and no provider-write path. Unknown rights remain a separate visible gate even if three page contexts are reviewed.

## Ownership, records and lifetime

**Candidate choice:** one worker instance is the sole authority for volatile selection decisions; the review page is a view and explicit-intent source, never a recovery authority. Ordinary worker suspension therefore sacrifices availability: a new worker requires all three fresh invocations. A long-lived port, open page or keepalive is not a guarantee of worker survival. This intentionally extends BF-04's fail-closed rule rather than introducing storage.

| Transient non-secret record | Owner and contents | Lifetime / constraints |
|---|---|---|
| Review context | Worker: unpredictable local `bootEpoch`, `reviewEpoch`, global `contextGeneration`, owning packaged review-document instance, mode | One live worker/review only; not Chrome profile identity, a clock, bearer credential or persisted run ID |
| Invocation candidate | Worker: local capture ID, genuine invoking tab/window, main-frame document ID when available, reviewed origin, status, request sequence | One outstanding unassigned capture; a second capture replaces it. Never turn access success without a new genuine event into a capture |
| Role binding | Worker: role, tab/window IDs, frame 0, opaque document ID, local selection/navigation generations, safe page-kind and URL-comparison verdict, confirmation statuses, evidence tier/state | At most three; useful only in the same live context. No account/repository/customer identifiers, credentials or approval capability |
| Observation request | Worker: one-use request ID, expected epochs/generations/tab/document and exact bounded result schema | One bounded read; consumed once, then discarded. Old/cancelled/duplicate responses have no authority |
| UI snapshot | Review page: safe labels, states/reasons, false authority flags and matching worker/view epochs | Display only; cleared/marked unavailable on disconnect or epoch mismatch; cannot repopulate worker bindings |

No persistent Chrome profile ID is invented. Same-profile use is constrained to the extension's own browser context and explicit normal-window selection, with human confirmation of the intended profile. Do not enumerate profiles, infer Chrome/Google identity, cross normal/incognito boundaries, or equate equal window IDs with equal site accounts. Window moves invalidate this candidate's bindings.

There is **no `chrome.tabs.Tab.documentId` property**. Use actual opaque document IDs from supported scripting results, navigation APIs and message sender context; no fixture marker, UUID-format guess, URL or frame ID substitutes. Missing support means manual fallback. Chrome 106 is the relevant document-API floor, not a newly adopted product support baseline. See [C3](#official-chrome-source-register), [C4](#official-chrome-source-register), [C5](#official-chrome-source-register), [C7](#official-chrome-source-register).

Full URLs may contain private routes or session material. They are not binding-record fields or durable diagnostics. Exact URL comparison, including query/fragment, uses a narrowly scoped worker-local observation comparator and discards the values when the read settles or is invalidated. It does not retain stable URL hashes. A suspected session-bearing URL, unknown/sensitive route requiring unauthorized inspection, or inability to compare safely blocks collection and offers manual mode. No tokens or credential values may be captured to make comparison work. Rechecking uses new browser observations plus the uninterrupted lifecycle generation, not a saved sensitive URL or old snapshot.

No storage API, checkpoint, database schema or retention duration is selected. Clear candidates/comparators on completion, cancellation, replacement or invalidation; clear all bindings on review loss or restart. “Short-lived” is bounded by this transaction/review lifetime, not a security TTL. Exact future read timeout and payload limits require a separately authorized bounded-read contract; absence of that contract means no runtime collector is authorized. Event/sequence generations are correlation markers, not trusted time or durable replay protection.

## Candidate state machine

The states apply to each candidate/role slot, with an unassigned capture attached to the current review. Initially all role slots are `UNBOUND`. A captured event can exist without occupying a role. Only explicit UI assignment transfers that capture into a role's `CONTEXT_UNVERIFIED` slot. This avoids treating an inferred hostname as a role choice.

| State | Required evidence / permitted activity | Prohibited activity |
|---|---|---|
| UNBOUND | No accepted binding; explain scope, accept a genuine new invocation or manual choice | Reconstruct from cached IDs, old UI, surviving scripting access or session restoration |
| INVOCATION_OBSERVED | Actual Chrome action event captured this tab in the current boot/review; bounded metadata acquisition may proceed | Automatic role assignment; treating event alone as a verified page/account |
| CONTEXT_UNVERIFIED | Explicit role/target intent plus current candidate, or deliberate recheck of an existing same-context binding; perform one bounded fresh observation | Aggregate ready status, account claim or any write |
| CONTEXT_VERIFIED_READ_ONLY | Complete observation transaction and explicit role/target confirmation; show only scoped RO context status | Treating observation as a lock, rights, approval or permission for a later read without rechecking |
| STALE_OR_UNCERTAIN | Known invalidation or insufficient/conflicting evidence; immediately stop scheduling, discard requests/comparators and clear aggregate status | Accepting queued results, retry/replay, restoring validity on grant success or URL return |
| RESELECTION_REQUIRED | Old binding unusable; safe generic reason shown; await new human invocation and confirmation | Auto-rebind old/new tab or skip invocation because access remains |
| MANUAL_FALLBACK | Automated observation stopped and bindings cleared; human works on native sites within their authority; any reported completion is MANUALLY_ATTESTED | Background reads, credential/API workaround or manual attestation promoted to automation authority |

The transition table is exhaustive. Invalid transitions return a sanitized refusal without state advancement. No transition authorizes a write. Self-display does not change state. Validity is never inferred from absence of events.

| From | Trigger and guard | To / mandatory effects |
|---|---|---|
| UNBOUND or RESELECTION_REQUIRED | Fresh actual human invocation; capture Chrome-supplied eligible tab in current worker/review | INVOCATION_OBSERVED for the unassigned capture; no role yet |
| INVOCATION_OBSERVED | Current capture passes initial browser-origin/access checks; trusted UI explicitly assigns unique role and target | CONTEXT_UNVERIFIED for that role; consume capture once, increment selection generation |
| CONTEXT_UNVERIFIED | Entire fresh observation transaction succeeds with unchanged generations and explicit confirmation | CONTEXT_VERIFIED_READ_ONLY; retain only bounded non-secret binding |
| CONTEXT_VERIFIED_READ_ONLY | Human requests another bounded RO check while worker/review/lifecycle coverage is continuous | CONTEXT_UNVERIFIED before dispatch; old verified display withdrawn; successful transaction required again |
| INVOCATION_OBSERVED, CONTEXT_UNVERIFIED or CONTEXT_VERIFIED_READ_ONLY | Selected/captured context invalidates, denial/timeout, missing field, target ambiguity or observation gap | STALE_OR_UNCERTAIN; execute global invalidation below |
| STALE_OR_UNCERTAIN | Invalidation completed; no pending request or usable old binding remains | RESELECTION_REQUIRED; never directly back to verified |
| Any state except MANUAL_FALLBACK | Human chooses manual mode, required capability unsupported/denied, or no safe authorized observation exists | MANUAL_FALLBACK; globally clear observation context and pending work |
| MANUAL_FALLBACK | Explicit human starts a new review, subject to unchanged gates | UNBOUND for all roles with a new review epoch; fresh invocations required |
| Any state | Human cancels/stops the review, review closes/navigates/reloads, owning UI connection is lost, or extension unload/update | UNBOUND for all roles; revoke application acceptance of all old messages; no claim of Chrome grant revocation |
| Any old process state | Worker/browser restart or unknown boot/session continuity | New process initializes every role UNBOUND; old UI must display reselection needed, then fresh review/invocations; never import prior state |

Changing an assigned role, selected tab, expected origin or target first invalidates the whole review: STALE_OR_UNCERTAIN → RESELECTION_REQUIRED. The capture that caused a conflict cannot silently overwrite an existing binding. After the old context is discarded, require fresh invocation/assignment; changing a role is not a shortcut around uniqueness. An unrelated unassigned invocation may supply the next missing role without invalidating already reviewed slots, but cannot grant aggregate readiness before all checks pass.

**Global invalidation rule:** any captured/selected-context uncertainty increments `contextGeneration` before awaiting anything, consumes/discards every pending request, clears the aggregate verdict and confirmation statuses, and invalidates all occupied role slots through STALE_OR_UNCERTAIN → RESELECTION_REQUIRED. Empty slots stay UNBOUND. All three roles must be selected/confirmed again before an aggregate verdict. This conservative candidate deliberately costs three new invocations after selected-context drift; an optimization retaining unaffected bindings would need a later design review. No partial stale context is usable.

Aggregate RO predicate: same live worker/review/context generation; three distinct explicitly selected role/tab bindings at distinct reviewed origins; every role CONTEXT_VERIFIED_READ_ONLY; matching current document/window/lifecycle and usable bounded read; no pending invalidation, read or ambiguity; guided lifecycle coverage available. Immediately before displaying the aggregate verdict, recheck the complete tuple; any event or new read withdraws it. This is a momentary observation verdict, never a continuously fresh account guarantee or an atomic cross-tab snapshot.

## Fresh observation and message acceptance contract

The following is prospective sequencing, not executable code or an adapter schema change.

1. Register relevant lifecycle/loss listeners before accepting capture; start with empty state. If listener coverage or continuity cannot be established, refuse guided binding. Do not infer a navigation barrier from a sleep, page-ready flag or empty event queue.
2. Capture current worker/review/context/selection/navigation generations and create one single-use request. Serialize selection changes and observation acceptance in the trusted worker. Confirm the owning review document is still the same trusted packaged instance.
3. Read current selected tab/window and browser top-frame context: eligible reviewed HTTPS origin, committed full URL, active main document, complete/not-discarded state and no known pending navigation. `pendingUrl` absence alone does not prove no navigation. Cross-check navigation frame information and usable document identity. Initial metadata discovery is followed by document-targeted observation; any mismatch discards the discovery.
4. Run only the separately reviewed packaged isolated-world, top-document bounded read; once known, target `{tabId, documentIds: [expectedDocumentId]}`. Do not combine documentIds with frameIds, use allFrames, or fall back to tab-only injection after document-target failure. A first discovery read cannot itself establish verified context. Required access and document-target semantics come from [C2](#official-chrome-source-register)/[C3](#official-chrome-source-register).
5. After **every await**, verify the saved generations, current selected IDs, owning UI, permission availability and active document/URL again. Require read result/document agreement and exact pre/post URL equality, including path/query/fragment, with no observed intervening navigation. A successful `permissions.contains` for the named activeTab permission does not prove a per-tab usable grant. API rejection, missing metadata or lifecycle disagreement fails closed; no automatic retry or permission escalation.
6. Accept a result exactly once for the pending request; reject late, duplicate, unsolicited or out-of-order results. Actual scripting results arrive through the extension's API completion path. Any content-script messages additionally require Chrome-supplied sender extension ID, selected tab/window, frame 0, document ID, URL/origin and exact bounded inert payload to match current worker expectations. Payload claims cannot establish sender identity. Unselected/opaque/subframe senders are dropped before privileged work. A sender port's original lifecycle field does not prove current activity. [C6](#official-chrome-source-register), [C7](#official-chrome-source-register).
7. UI intents require the exact packaged review URL, extension ID, owning browser document/context instance, review epoch, current generation and a permitted bounded intent. Content messages cannot assign roles, approve operations, choose arbitrary hosts/selectors or invoke commands. Render observations as text. An old review page cannot take ownership by presenting a remembered epoch.
8. Confirm role/target intent still applies, record only the non-secret verdict and dispose of comparator data. On mismatched current-selected evidence, invalidate globally; unrelated malformed/old messages are rejected without supplying authority. Stale messages can never validate a newer binding. Conservatively invalidating on ambiguous selected events is acceptable; treating ambiguity as fresh evidence is not.

These checks cannot make Chrome calls, DOM changes, user review and three-site state atomic. Navigation events from different APIs may be delivered in different orders; delayed events may force needless reselection. Same-document account/target changes may leave URL/document unchanged. No event scheme, nonce, URL equality, document-target success or manual account attestation eliminates that race or authenticates a hostile DOM. Unknown page/account/target means manual fallback; future real-site observation contracts must establish what can safely be checked. No later write may rely on this read verdict alone.

## Invalidation and manual recovery contract

All “invalidate” outcomes use the global rule above. Browser capability can survive an application invalidation; application stopping does not revoke site sessions or undo external effects.

| Trigger / signal | Required safe response | Recovery and prohibited inference |
|---|---|---|
| Main-frame navigation start, reload, commit or navigation error | Invalidate at earliest observed start/loading, even same URL/origin; do not wait for completion | Fresh human invocation and complete observation after stable destination; same-origin surviving grant is insufficient |
| History/fragment/query change, back/forward or same-URL history event | Invalidate even if document ID and final URL match old values | Fresh selection; away-and-back and BFCache cannot restore a verdict |
| Cross-origin redirect, login or custom host | Invalidate before further collection; refuse unreviewed destination | Human handles site sign-in/challenge; separately review origin and invoke again. Returning to old origin does not revive binding or grant |
| Tab closed/window closed, `tabs.onRemoved` | Invalidate; reject old ID and all in-flight results | Human selects an existing intended tab or opens a replacement themselves; same URL never transfers a binding |
| `tabs.onReplaced`, navigation replacement/prerender activation | Invalidate removed and added context; no ID remapping | Fresh selection; BF-05-R1 close/reopen is not genuine replacement evidence |
| Tab detach/attach/move, unexpected window or normal/incognito context | Invalidate; no transfer between windows or browser contexts | Human confirms intended normal-profile tab/window; never invent persistent profile identity |
| Duplicate tabs | Keep explicit unique selected ID; leave unselected duplicate unread | Explicit replacement requires invalidation/reselection; no hostname/most-recent/first-match substitution |
| Grant loss, withheld access, managed denial, optional permission removal or API failure | Invalidate, stop new reads and reject queued results; grant-added event alone changes no binding | New human invocation/consent only within separately approved scope, or manual mode. Do not treat permission query or removal refusal as actual revocation |
| Optional navigation capability absent/lost | Stop guided three-role observation; manual fallback | BF-04's fresh single-tab/manual option only; no polling substitute or broad host request |
| Popup closes / loses focus | No popup-owned authority; abandon any popup-only unfinished intent | Actual popup lifecycle remains unverified. Proposed no-popup action path must not depend on popup survival |
| Owning review page closes/reloads/navigates or disconnects | Clear all state; reopening shows UNBOUND/reselection needed | No rehydration from page cache or still-usable grant. Switching focus to invoke another tab is normal and alone is not review closure |
| Worker stops/restarts, including natural idle or unexpected loss | Old worker loses bindings; new boot starts UNBOUND before accepting messages. UI cannot return its cache to authority | Require a fresh review and three new genuine invocations even if a bounded scripting check could still succeed |
| Browser restart/crash or uncertain session restoration | Treat all old tab/document/session associations as untrusted; UNBOUND | Never equate restored tabs/IDs/access with verified context; no automatic resume or reapproval |
| Late message, old document, request timeout, generation mismatch or racing selection/navigation | Reject result; selected-context uncertainty invalidates | No result relabeling, automatic retry, clock-based revival or acceptance from matching URL alone |
| Same-document account/target switch, logout, ambiguous account/rights, unsupported page/iframe | Invalidate on observed/known uncertainty; do not scrape credentials or infer from login appearance | Human verifies native site; missing reliable authorized evidence means manual fallback. Account rights remain unverified |
| Restricted `chrome://`, Web Store/protected page, opaque URL, missing document support | No injection bypass; manual fallback | Invoke an eligible approved website tab if available; no debugger, MAIN-world workaround or new permission by inference |
| Human cancels or stops | Consume pending requests, clear all bindings and verdicts; UNBOUND | No automatic tab closure/navigation or website logout; explain any Chrome access that remains |
| Human changes role/tab/target/origin | Invalidate before accepting replacement intent | Fresh selections for all roles; new role choice cannot carry any old approval |
| Possible write discovered during future recovery | Stop; preserve independent `RECONCILE_REQUIRED` obligation under protected policy | BF-06 has no writes. Reselection/manual mode/new review cannot clear unknown outcome, trigger retry or bypass SEC-01 |

Manual recovery is deliberately simple: stop extension observation; show only generic reason and affected role labels; human checks the intended profile and native website account/target; chooses manual completion or starts a fresh review with new invocations. Do not auto-open replacements, re-login, fill forms or retry. Manual attestation supplies no automated account verification. Explain that clearing application bindings does not revoke each activeTab grant; Chrome settings/navigation/closure have separate effects. Do not claim optional removal succeeded without its observed result. [C2](#official-chrome-source-register), [C9](#official-chrome-source-register).

## Security, privacy and future authority gates

No credentials, cookies, tokens, session extraction, private page globals, credential storage inspection, screenshots, network interception, authenticated fetch/XHR client, undocumented endpoint, OAuth/backend or provider write. Page metadata collection is prospective and read-only; Chrome's scripting capability itself is not a read-only permission. Packaged code must enforce that restriction; form filling can autosave and is outside observation. Isolated globals do not make shared DOM trustworthy. [C10](#official-chrome-source-register).

Minimal future diagnostics: role, generic state/reason, operation category such as observation, evidence tier and aggregate counts. No raw/full URLs, query/fragment, customer-specific origin in logs, account/repository/customer values, title/favicon, DOM/page text, raw errors/messages, document/tab IDs, stable identifier hashes, screenshots, telemetry/export or clipboard capture. Safe origins may be displayed locally only after the privacy review; native-site viewing handles sensitive target details. No durable diagnostic store or logging retention is authorized.

[SEC-01](../phase04/PHASE_04_SEC01_APPROVED_POLICY.md), the [clock-security packet](../phase04/PHASE_04_SEC01_CLOCK_SECURITY_ACCEPTANCE_PACKET.md), [restart restrictions](../phase04/PHASE_04_SEC01_STALLED_WALL_AND_RESTART_RESOLUTION.md) and [cleanup outcome blocker](../phase04/PHASE_04_SEC01_CLEANUP_OUTCOME_CONTRACT_DECISION.md) remain untouched. No eighth field, alternate storage, elapsed-time claim, cleanup retry, uncertain-hold deletion, fresh-run bypass or policy modification. Volatile tab records are not persisted checkpoints or a substitute for blocked durable recovery. New review epochs cannot clear existing uncertainty or outstanding retention obligations.

[Phase 06](../phase06/PHASE_06.md) approval intent remains disconnected, SIMULATED and `executionAuthorized: false`. No durable duplicate-write guarantee, automatic recovery/resume/retry/replay or browser executor is designed here. A future write architecture requires separately authorized decisions for real site/account/rights evidence, permitted use, exact target/effect, immediate per-write trusted approval, consumption/invalidation of that approval, post-result verification and guarded reconciliation/SEC-01 integration. These gates cannot be satisfied by synthesizing a Phase 06 record or by surviving Chrome access. Until then there is no runtime write authority.
