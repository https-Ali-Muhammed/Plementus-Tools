## Approved operational rules

1. **Fail closed.** A checkpoint with detected or unresolved time uncertainty cannot automatically resume, transition, authorize an action, or trigger retry or replay. Detectable rollback, unexplained forward movement, unresolved worker/browser restart trust, missing time, invalid/legacy data, cleanup failure/conflict and a possible external write require a generic blocked result until the applicable condition is resolved under this policy. A clock reset, plausible timestamp, checkbox or approval cannot establish elapsed time. Exact detection criteria remain an implementation/security gate.
2. **Account for exceptional retention.** Keep a blocked record local while clock uncertainty, cleanup failure or guarded resolution remains outstanding. Track it as an outstanding physical-retention obligation through a sanitized owner-controlled process; never call an attempted or failed cleanup a completed deletion. There is **no guaranteed maximum physical hold period**. This exception does not authorize ignoring records. Normal seven-day logical expiry and next-permitted-execution cleanup under [Retention Option B](PHASE_04_SEC01_RETENTION_AMENDMENT_B.md) still apply when time can be trusted.
3. **Require the project owner for destructive disposition.** When expiry cannot be established, only explicit, case-specific **project-owner approval** may authorize manual deletion. **No delegation is approved.** Approval covers the identified record and disposition, not a finding that seven days elapsed and not permission to resume the old checkpoint.
4. **Check evidence and transaction outcome.** Before deletion, the owner must receive sanitized evidence of the blocked condition, target identity and expected revision where reliable, prior cleanup outcome, possible external-write reconciliation, and any incident-evidence hold. The guarded service must recheck authority, target and revision where available, perform an authorized transaction, confirm completion, and verify absence where feasible. A failed absence check or uncertain completion remains unresolved. Do not expose record contents, customer details, credentials, URLs, raw errors or screenshots in the review or logs. Do not add a persisted checkpoint field.
5. **Preserve conflicts and invalid rows.** A stale revision, abort or failed deletion leaves the record blocked and the physical-retention obligation open; never report deletion as complete. Missing or unreliable identity/revision evidence cannot be bypassed. A separate reviewed procedure is required before disposition of a corrupt or legacy row that lacks reliable guards. Do not infer its expiry, silently repair it, migrate it or automatically discard it.
6. **Reconcile external effects and authorize new work.** Inspect the actual outcome of a possible external write through an authorized reconciliation path before any new action. Never automatically retry or replay the uncertain action. A new workflow requires fresh approval and current target, account, origin, environment and permission checks. An uncertain old checkpoint cannot resume unless separately sufficient evidence and security approval justify it; manual deletion approval does not provide either.
7. **Leave blocked when the owner is unavailable.** Retain the checkpoint and its outstanding obligation. No substitute authority, automatic disposal or escalation deadline is approved. Preserve necessary incident evidence before destructive disposition; incident contacts, notification route and release procedure remain activation prerequisites. IndexedDB absence does not prove forensic erasure.

The operator sees only a generic **checkpoint blocked; owner review required** state and sanitized cleanup **not attempted / outstanding / committed** status. The project owner receives only the sanitized case evidence needed to decide. The seven persisted checkpoint fields remain exactly `schemaVersion`, `runId`, `state`, `step`, `evidenceTier`, `revision`, `createdAt`; storage remains browser-profile-local and accessible only through the guarded extension service. No customer or credential persistence, telemetry, export, backend, remote time service, new permission or additional field is approved.

## Physical-hold limit and policy relationship

The owner approved an exception without claiming an enforceable maximum physical hold. The seven-field browser-only design cannot prove elapsed time across an unobserved restart, and Chrome may remain closed or receive no service event. A local alarm or local timestamp cannot guarantee deletion by a real-time deadline when the clock is untrusted. The [approved uncertainty amendment](PHASE_04_SEC01_UNCERTAIN_TIME_RETENTION_AMENDMENT.md) permits local retention while the record remains blocked and under owner-controlled review, without weakening normal trusted-time cleanup. It does not establish clock integrity, permit automatic deletion of uncertain records, or authorize ignoring an outstanding obligation.
