Phase 04 is **IN_PROGRESS / BLOCKED**; progress is **4/20 master phases and 18/72 micro-phases accepted**; production activation is **NO_GO**.

## Approved exception and boundaries

- Normal logical expiry remains exactly seven days after immutable `createdAt`. When time can be trusted, attempt physical cleanup at the next permitted extension execution after expiry, before checkpoint-dependent work. A closed browser cannot execute cleanup. Neither update nor manual review renews expiry.
- When expiry cannot safely be established, or a guarded cleanup transaction fails or conflicts, block checkpoint use and record an outstanding physical-retention obligation through a sanitized, owner-controlled process. Exceptional local retention is permitted while clock uncertainty, cleanup failure or guarded resolution remains outstanding. No guaranteed maximum physical hold period is claimed, and the exception does not permit ignoring an outstanding record.
- Do not delete solely on uncertain expiry, treat an attempt or failed transaction as completed deletion, or automatically resume, transition, authorize an action, retry or replay from an uncertain checkpoint.
- If expiry remains unprovable, manual deletion is a destructive disposition requiring **explicit, case-specific project-owner approval**. No delegation is approved. Approval does not prove elapsed time or authorize use of the old checkpoint. Require sanitized evidence, reliable target and revision checks where available, an authorized guarded transaction, confirmed completion and feasible absence verification. An uncertain or failed result leaves the retention obligation open.
- Missing or unreliable identity/revision evidence cannot be bypassed; a separately reviewed procedure is required before disposition. Preserve necessary incident evidence before destructive deletion. If the project owner is unavailable, keep the record blocked and retained; no alternate authority, automatic disposal or escalation deadline is approved.
- Reconcile a possible external write before new action. Never automatically retry or replay it. New work requires fresh authorization and target verification. An uncertain old checkpoint cannot resume without separately sufficient evidence and security approval.

The seven persisted fields remain exactly `schemaVersion`, `runId`, `state`, `step`, `evidenceTier`, `revision`, `createdAt`. Device/browser-profile-local storage, one guarded service, no customer or credential persistence, no sync/export/telemetry, and the other approved privacy and security restrictions remain in force. IndexedDB deletion does not prove forensic erasure. This amendment approves no backend, remote time source, new field, permission, implementation, browser write or activation.

## Open activation gates

The browser-only schema cannot prove elapsed time across an unobserved restart or enforce a real-time maximum physical hold when the local clock is untrusted. Exact uncertainty-detection criteria, actual owner identity and request checks, browser-worker integration, guarded recovery paths, device controls, incident contacts and notification route, security review, synthetic tests and isolated real-browser verification remain **OPEN / NOT VERIFIED**. Policy approval alone closes none of these gates. Production activation remains **NO_GO**.
